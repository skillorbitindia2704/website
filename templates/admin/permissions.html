Rate Limiting & Lockout

Accounts automatically lock for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive failed login attempts via failed_login_attempts and locked_until timestamps.

CSRF & Integrity

Every state-modifying POST request validates cryptographic CSRF tokens, preventing cross-site tampering and unauthorized privilege escalations.
