Administrator Security Best Practices

  • Always use strong unique passwords with at least 8 characters including letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Never share administrator login credentials among multiple staff members; create separate accounts for audit trails.
  • Promptly demote or disable admin accounts for team members who no longer require platform access.
  • The system requires at least one active administrator account at all times to prevent administrative lockout.
